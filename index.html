Shortcuts

        

          Alt+F food, W water, D dog food, R record, B book,
          T pat, P phone, A alarm, C games, L letters,
          M sound on/off, Enter full screen. Alt+N debug panel, Alt+G floor graph.
        

        

          URL: ?seed=123, ?speed=1 (starting speed; default 30), ?quick, ?movedIn, ?hour=14 (new game's starting time),
          ?fresh (ignore the save and start again; it will be overwritten). After 30 minutes with no clicks or keys, time slows to real time until you're back (?idle=60 sets the wait in seconds).
          Dev: ?zoom=4&x=0&y=0 close-up, ?debug exposes lcp in the console.
